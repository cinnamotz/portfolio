Classic City Guide is a high-fidelity mobile app prototype designed for visitors exploring Athens, Georgia. Built in Figma as part of a UX design course at the University of Georgia, the app features curated restaurant and hotel recommendations with a clean, intuitive browsing experience designed around the needs of first-time visitors to the Classic City. The project demonstrates a full end-to-end design process — from initial wireframing and component system development through interactive prototyping and usability testing with real users

B usiness P rocess T ransformation

ImagineX came to us with 11 disconnected repositories, no single source of truth, and a staffing process that cost $137.67 in senior leadership time every time someone needed to find a qualified candidate. Our team mapped the operational breakdown, quantified the cost, and proposed an AI-Readiness SSOT Dashboard built on an API orchestration layer and a tiered skills model. We presented directly to the client board, who adopted our recommendation to hire interns to build and maintain the infrastructure — projecting $14,640 in annual savings and a 70%+ reduction in staffing search costs.

H ate C rime A nalysis

Using 4,000+ NYPD hate crime records from Data.gov, we built a Tableau dashboard analyzing hate crime patterns across NYC 's five boroughs from 2019–2024. Key findings included a post-COVID surge peaking in 2024, a 43% rise in Anti-Jewish bias complaints, and a 60–70% citywide enforcement gap — with Anti-Jewish crimes carrying the lowest arrest rates (29–40%) despite being the most frequently reported category, a paradox driven by the nature of the offenses themselves (vandalism and harassment vs. physical confrontation).
