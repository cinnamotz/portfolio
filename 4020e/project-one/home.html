Stray Kids

Stray Kids is a South Korean boy group formed by JYP Entertainment. The group consists of eight members: Bang Chan, Lee Know, Changbin, Hyunjin, HAN, Felix, Seungmin, and I.N.

Known for energetic performances and personal involvement in their music, Stray Kids have developed a sound that combines hip-hop, electronic music, pop, rock, and experimental elements.
