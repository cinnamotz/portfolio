Anime and Kpop Journaling

What is it?

Anime and Kpop journaling is my personal way of documenting the different media I consume. I watch a lot of different anime, ranging from One Piece to Medalist, and I listen to a lot of Kpop, with my favorite band being Stray Kids. I enjoy documenting the different seasons and albums that I listen to with a collage of photographs, stickers, doodles and tape to create pages that best reflect the aesthetic of the show or the album I'm journaling about.

Why do I journal?

I journal as a way to let off steam and express myself creatively without needed a screen. I enjoy how therapeutic it is to cut up old extra albums and photobooks I have in order to create a piece of art that's unique to me and to the media I'm documenting. It also helps me reflect on why I like the media that I consume and keeps me grounded to reality when I get too caught up in the media.

What do I use?

I use a variety of different materials to create my pages, including:

  • Old albums and photobooks
  • Stickers
  • Washi tape
  • Tape roller
  • Scissors
  • Markers and pens
I also have a sticker cutting machine, where I make my own "deco" stickers to use in my journal. Deco stickers are usually used as page fillers and are simple shapes like concentric circles and squares and stars.
